# Foundations

Moji is a living extraction layer, not an upfront encyclopedia.

## Brand Principles

- Warm: customer-facing surfaces should feel welcoming without becoming childish.
- Fast: the next action should be obvious and reachable.
- Trustworthy: payment, bill, status, and split flows prioritize clarity.
- Mobile-first: diner surfaces are designed for touch and small screens first.

## Visual Principles

- Prefer semantic decisions over hardcoded styles.
- Use clear hierarchy before decoration.
- Use borders, radius, spacing, and surface changes before shadows.
- Make status readable through text and icons, not color alone.
- Prioritize legibility, scanability, and action clarity.

## Surface Philosophy

- Diner Ordering Flow: mobile-first, comfortable, direct, and warm.
- Dashboard: operational, dense, and scan-heavy; draft until audited.
- Auth: calm, narrow, and reassuring; draft until polished.
- Billing/Analytics/Staff/Loyalty: roadmap-only until extracted from real UI.
