# Patterns

Patterns describe how tokens and components combine inside real product surfaces.

## Diner Ordering Flow

The diner flow is the first complete surface.

Included patterns:

- Menu browsing.
- Item customization sheet.
- Cart and checkout.
- Live orders and order status.
- Bill, payment, and split bill.
- Phone capture and loyalty prompts.
- Empty, loading, error, and success states.

The diner layout should preserve a phone-like ordering shell on desktop rather than becoming a wide multi-column dashboard.

## Bottom Action Pattern

Use a fixed CTA footer when the main next action must remain reachable.

Rules:

- Content scrolls above the footer.
- Footer has a top border and white surface.
- CTA is fully rounded and at least 48px tall.
- Safe-area bottom spacing is respected.
- Last content item remains visible and tappable above the footer.

## Card Family Pattern

Menu item cards, cart item rows, order items, payment cards, and split bill cards should feel related.

Shared traits:

- White surface.
- Subtle border.
- Rounded outer container.
- Clear title/secondary/price hierarchy.
- Pill actions and compact status indicators.

## Future Surfaces

Dashboard, auth, analytics, staff, settings, billing, and loyalty should remain roadmap placeholders until each module is polished from real UI.

Do not finalize future module rules ahead of their audits.
